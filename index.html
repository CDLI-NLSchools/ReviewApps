About the Review Apps

What are the Review Apps?

Practice question banks for the courses above. Pick your course, then practise by unit or by curriculum indicator. Most questions are generated new every time, so you can practise the same skill again and get a different question. You can work online, print a practice sheet, or build a practice exam.

Who are they for?

Students in any of the courses above: whether you are keeping up with class, catching up on a topic, or getting ready for a test or exam. Teachers can also build practice sets and printable sheets for their classes, and export fully editable versions into Word or Brightspace.

When should I use them?

A little at a time, often. Try a short set after a lesson, a few questions as part of homework, and some mixed review in the days before a test. Short sessions spread over several days work better than one long session the night before.

Where can I use them?

Anywhere you have a browser: at school, at home, or on the go. The apps work on Chromebooks, laptops, tablets and desktops, and you can also print practice sheets for working offline.

Why does practice work?

Trying to remember an answer, instead of looking it up, is one of the strongest ways to learn. Every time you pull an answer out of your own memory, that memory gets stronger and lasts longer. Checking your work right away, with a worked solution to compare against, also catches mistakes before they turn into habits.

How should I practise?
  1. Pick one unit or indicator and start a set.
  2. Try each question on your own first, and write your work out. It feels harder than peeking at the example, but that effort is what builds the memory.
  3. Stuck? Use the hint first, then the worked example, then try the question again.
  4. Check each answer as you go and read the full solution, even when you are right. Notice the one step you would do differently next time.
  5. Come back in a day or two and practise the same skill again. Spacing your practice out is what moves it into long-term memory.
