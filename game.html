Review Rally is a pilot game built on the Review Apps question banks. Students play under generated names; no student accounts are created and no real names are entered. Rooms are temporary and are deleted when the teacher ends the game. A student who wants their results recorded can enter their class ID number when they join; it is stored encrypted in the room, shown on no screen, and only their teacher can match it to them.
