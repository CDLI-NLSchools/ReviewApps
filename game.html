Review Rally is a pilot game built on the Review Apps question banks. Students play under generated names; no student accounts are created and no real names are entered. Rooms are temporary and are deleted when the teacher ends the game. A student who wants their results recorded can share them at the end with their class ID number, which only their teacher can match to them.
